# Vaulted Deep

> Vaulted Deep is a permanent carbon removal and organic waste disposal company. It turns carbon-filled organic waste into a slurry and injects it thousands of feet underground using patented geologic slurry sequestration technology adapted from oil and gas practice, trapping the carbon permanently beneath impermeable rock. Spun out of Advantek Waste Management Services in 2023.

## What it does

- Waste streams accepted: biosolids from municipal wastewater treatment, agricultural byproducts and excess manure, pulp and paper sludge.
- Method: minimally processed sludgy organic waste is slurried and injected into deep rock formations, isolated by layers of steel and cement. Sequestration is geologic and durable rather than surface-based.
- Sites: Great Plains (Hutchinson, Kansas), operating since 2023; Terminal Island (Los Angeles, California), operating since 2008 and run with partner Advantek.
- Customers named publicly: City of Derby, Kansas; City of Los Angeles; Pratt Feeders.
- Commercial model: bilateral contracts — waste disposal capacity for utilities and agricultural operators, durable carbon removal credits for corporate buyers. No published price list.
- Internal tooling: the company markets an AI-accelerated Site Development Platform (subsurface suitability modeling, permit drafting assistance, waste mix optimization, real-time operations and injection analysis). It is described as how Vaulted Deep develops and runs its own sites, not as a product sold or exposed to third parties.

- No api., docs. or developers. subdomain resolves in DNS.
- The only machine-readable surface on the domain is the WordPress core REST index at https://vaulteddeep.com/wp-json/ — the default wp/v2 namespace plus plugin namespaces (Yoast, Wordfence, CookieYes, Redirection, FileBird, Font Awesome). It is CMS plumbing for a three-page marketing site, not a Vaulted Deep product API, and is deliberately not registered as one in this profile.
